# Prop pack guidance

Prefer references with minimal occlusion and at least one view that reveals depth. Put exact text, symbols, colors, materials, moving parts, and damage patterns in the brief; image analysis alone should not guess hidden construction.

The fixed views are front, left three-quarter, left side, rear, overhead, and a construction detail. These views support story continuity and art direction. They are not orthographic CAD, compliance photography, or evidence of real-world scale.

When multiple references conflict, stop before rendering and ask which one controls. If a review reports geometry or marking drift, keep the failed output and make a new explicit repair attempt when that workflow exists.
